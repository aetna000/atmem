# Whole-identifier evidence for structural context ranking

Bounded private ranking correction after the failed v3 token target, T088.
No new public API, persisted format, model, dependency or authority is added.

The all-case trace audit and nineteen exact frozen-pack replays reproduce a
generic false-match defect. Multiple arbitrary query substrings anywhere in a
symbol's leaf name receive name-level weight: `proper` matches `property` and
`rated` matches `decorated`. Incidental prose can therefore promote unrelated
source. The original installation/query bindings match their frozen inputs;
this is not repaired by changing benchmark labels or task identities.

## Correction and boundaries

Keep normal exact identifier, underscore and camel-case tokenization. Additional
compound matches are allowed only when at least two distinct query terms, each
at least four characters long, completely segment one compact identifier part.
There may be no unexplained prefix, infix or suffix, and matching may not bridge
underscore or camel-case boundaries. Segmentation is deterministic and bounded
to 256-character compact parts; longer parts retain their normal exact-token
matching without additional compound expansion. For example, `storememory` may
match `store memory`, while `decorated_with_property` must not gain `proper` or
`rated`, and `profile` must not gain `file`.

Do not add repository names, issue IDs, gold-patch paths, test outcomes, special
query words from the failed cohort, or new numeric efficacy thresholds. Preserve
source hashes, original bytes, scope checks, snapshot freshness, graph ownership,
byte budgets, omission labels, exposure receipts, and the native-tool fallback.
The failed v3 wheel and every recorded result remain immutable.

## Verification and evaluation treatment

Require focused positive/negative lexical fixtures, an end-to-end no-spurious-
context fixture, existing byte/scope/tamper and packing tests, and the relevant
Code/knowledge regression suite. These fixtures are boundary tests, not training
data or a token-saving result. Recompute retained packs on the now-reused cohort
to inspect the correction, without claiming this is untouched confirmation.
Any model comparison must use a separately installed, frozen candidate and
explicit matched-run protocol. The active 50%/20% TOTAL-input and full-task quality
objective remains unchanged. No gain is assumed from this bug fix alone.
