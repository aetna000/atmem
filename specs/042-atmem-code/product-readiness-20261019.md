## Remaining release gates

The later installed [five-benefit controls](../../RandD/2026-10-19-code-five-benefits-controls.md)
complete the bounded cross-machine bundle check. They do not qualify live
two-CLI delivery, every platform dependency, or competitive task performance.

1. Ordinary per-host rich outcome capture/producer binding and actual new-session
   experience delivery. Prompt-only qualification does not upgrade these tiers.
2. Claimed Windows/Linux/Python-version installed tests, complete dependency
   SBOM/binary provenance and packaging/legal review. A universal wheel does not
   qualify native encryption dependencies everywhere.
3. Broader Explorer/browser accessibility journeys and the five-user formative
   study; new list controls are not a usability study or full reconstruction.
4. Frozen held-out coding evaluation with actual prior experience against strong
   native/native-memory controls. Existing Click results do not prove savings.
5. An unused final release version, aligned package/bridge/installer pins and
   coordinated release gates. No publication is authorized by this request.
