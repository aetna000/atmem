# Second loop: implementation localization, not heading matches

Continuation of the owner's active efficiency goal. The completed first loop
remains negative and immutable. This is a new bounded protocol, not a revised
interpretation of its scores.

Before any solver dispatch, qualify the installed artifact on a disjoint public
source tree through the actual pinned extractor and product context service:
named functions/methods must yield implementation lines, not just headings,
imports or outlines. Inspect exact scope/source receipts and a changed-source
withholding control. Record the corpus/license/revision and retained results.

The candidate fixes implementation-tier ranking, namespace-aware named-symbol
matching and explicitly partial source windows for large definitions. Upstream
extraction and graph queries stay unchanged. Authorization, byte limits, hashes,
source rechecks, scope isolation and native tools stay mandatory.

Bound: one candidate, five familiar-task native/structure pairs (10 attempts),
then one newly selected five-task transfer cohort (10 attempts) if qualified.
No paid API/GPU. Existing Luna subscription and the same CLI/limits/instructions.
Native controls are fresh. Tuning requires 5/5 functional/policy passes, equal
or better than native, plus 50% total input-token saving or the 20% fallback.
Transfer must independently meet the paired quality/token threshold. Output,
uncached input, latency, all-budget failures and uncertainty remain reported.

The first-loop transfer tasks may diagnose defects but are retired as untouched
confirmation. Select/qualify a different transfer set before its solver runs,
record eligibility exclusions, and freeze the final candidate before scoring it.
No changes based on that transfer's outcomes; no best-of-task pooling, hidden
grade feedback, silent retries, task replacements after solving or target-ID
rules in the product. All old/new artifacts, failures and exact-event encrypted
archives remain separate. MEM remains benchmark-only; private keys internal.

This is still a small engineering study, not production-scale or general savings
evidence. No main-branch changes, owner installation, release, training or Laya.
