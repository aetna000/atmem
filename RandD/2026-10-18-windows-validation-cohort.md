### Interpretation and next gate

The predeclared aggregate full-task/input validation gate passes for pack +
tools at 20%, not 50%; pack alone fails the input gate. This is a small selected
validation result, not statistical equivalence, untouched confirmation or proof
that graph tools caused the reduction. Only one Code MCP call occurs, and the
tools arm uses fewer tokens in only five of ten matched pairs. Partial target
correctness is lower and cold component-sum latency is higher than native.
Do not market this as an unqualified quality-preserving speedup.

Freeze the candidate and analysis before a new-repository confirmation batch;
do not tune on these private grades or rerun these same attempts until they win.
The product preview wheel and exact model/caps remain those recorded above.
The original implementation, failed setup and administrative recovery records
remain available. T062/T077 and the overall goal remain open pending independent
confirmation and archive recovery.
