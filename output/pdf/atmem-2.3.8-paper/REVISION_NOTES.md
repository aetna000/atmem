# Scientific revision notes

This revision responds to external rejection-risk feedback without inventing
new measurements or hiding the status of the evaluated partitions.

| Concern | Manuscript change | Remaining evidence requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Inspected benchmark subsets cannot establish generalization | Reframed as a systems-design and formative-evaluation paper; removed comparative scores from the title, abstract, contribution statement and conclusion | Execute the frozen confirmation protocol before making a generalization claim |
| Low absolute scores and large verified-evidence gap | Made the 14-question evidence-delivery gap the central utility finding; redesigned the main result figure around absolute outcomes | Improve and confirm evidence delivery on untouched cases |
| Weak comparator configurations | Moved local Mem0 and AgentRunbook-R rows to a clearly labeled exploratory appendix | Run recommended strong and resource-matched configurations |
| No component attribution | Replaced causal language with mechanism hypotheses and retained an explicit ablation matrix | Complete all frozen ablations under matched conditions |
| Limited theoretical novelty | Recast propositions as standard conditional design checks rather than a theoretical contribution | None; the contribution is systems integration |
| Preliminary integrity evidence | Described the AGMI maintainer run as third-party conformance evidence, not certification or universal enforcement | Independent clean-room lifecycle and host-enforcement study |
| Incomplete reproducibility | Added pre-existing confirmation split artifacts to the evidence snapshot; documented mounted public corpora and exact source-to-claim identities | Publish case-level historical traces where licenses permit; all future confirmation traces are mandatory |
| Overbroad framing | Retitled and rewrote the paper around governed evidence assembly and failure observability | Do not restore superiority language without confirmation evidence |
| Draft disclaimer undermined readiness | Replaced it with a standard authorship, AI-assistance and peer-review disclosure | Author must verify affiliation before external submission |

The revision does not relabel any prior run as held out, independent, strong-
baseline, causal, statistically significant, or production evidence.
